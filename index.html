About the Lab

We build intelligent systems that turn complex, real-world data — from wearable sensors to satellite imagery — into tools for better health and a safer environment. Our work draws on artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and large language models, applied to two interconnected domains: Health, Environment.

1. Health and Biomedical Technologies

The core of our work lies in Health-Tech, where we develop AI and data-driven models using wearable sensors for the objective assessment and monitoring of individuals. Our systems enable early diagnosis, tracking, and rehabilitation of neurological disorders such as Parkinson’s disease and post-stroke recovery. We also advance intelligent methods for elderly care and cardiovascular monitoring, integrating multi-sensor data fusion and predictive analytics to enhance precision medicine and personalized healthcare.

2. Environmental and Disaster Technology

We apply AI and remote sensing to environmental monitoring and disaster response. Current and past work includes deforestation detection from aerial imagery using deep learning, wildfire smoke and fire classification developed in collaboration with Pakistan's National Disaster Management Authority, and an ongoing research partnership with the University of Halmstad, Sweden and the University of Glasgow, UK on AI for disaster management. This work bridges data science and environmental sustainability through intelligent monitoring designed for early warning and response. By keeping intelligent systems at the core while working across these two domains, our research aims to move computational methods out of the lab and into tools that protect people and the environments they live in.

By maintaining a core focus on intelligent systems while integrating these diverse application areas, our research strives to translate cutting-edge computational methods into high-impact, real-world solutions that enhance health and environmental safety.

Core Research Areas

Our expertise is concentrated in the following interdisciplinary domains:

  • Expertise in wearable sensors, IMUs, and physiological monitoring for non-invasive assessment of human motion, sleep, and cardiovascular activity.
  • Developed AI-driven diagnostic models for Parkinson’s disease and post-stroke motor assessment using time-series physiological data.
  • Experience in large-scale multi-sensor data acquisition and processing in real-world clinical and ambient settings (e.g., iCARE-IT, DAPHNE, and E-Motions projects).
  • Modeling of cerebellar microcircuits, synaptic plasticity, and neural dynamics to simulate brain-inspired control mechanisms.
  • Application of spiking neural models and biologically realistic simulations (EBrains 2.0, CNS 2025).
  • Integration of neuroscience principles into adaptive robotic and AI systems for motor learning and sensory fusion.
  • Expertise in CNNs, RNNs, and LSTMs for image, time-series, and biomedical data analysis.
  • Hands-on experience with YOLO, VGGFace2, FaceNet, and transformer-based architectures for detection and recognition tasks.
  • Certified in Machine Learning for Time-Series Data (Python) and AI applications in health, surveillance, and robotics domains.
  • Proficient in Python, R, and MATLAB for data-driven modeling, statistical learning, and visualization.
  • Applied data mining and predictive analytics for healthcare, environmental monitoring, and security applications.
  • Publications in Frontiers in Big Data and Computational Intelligence and Neuroscience demonstrating interdisciplinary analytical approaches.
  • Design and implementation of AI- and IoT-enabled rehabilitation, assistive, and monitoring systems for elderly and neurodegenerative patients.
  • Collaboration in European projects such as ACCRA, KERAAL, and DAPHNE focusing on robot-mediated physical therapy and smart health ecosystems.
  • Emphasis on ethical, privacy-preserving, and human-in-the-loop AI for sustainable healthcare innovation.
